# Channel distribution and attribution boundaries

Read when: distributing educational content across owned channels, partnerships, newsletters or measuring their business contribution.

## Channel fit before replication
Decide by true audience evidence, channel context, verified rights, production/review cost and maintenance burden. A help article belongs in product help first; short platform-native excerpts may lead readers to the canonical owned resource. Don't automatically blast the same content to all networks.

| Real audience job | Distribution candidate | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Solve repeated service issue | Help center and contextual app link | No stale instructions |
| Implement integration | Developer docs and compliant technical community contribution | Actual tested code |
| Evaluate purchase | Grounded comparison and relevant partner channels | No fabricated advantage |
| Re-engage a subscriber | Approved lifecycle program | No send without permission |

A publishing strategy is **not** permission to send emails, harvest leads, install analytics trackers or push social posts. Handoff marketing operations to lifecycle-retention and privacy/legal gate.

## Metric contracts
Define reach, qualified engagement, completed goal and assisted outcome separately. UTM campaign source is **descriptive attribution**, not proof that the content caused the conversion. Record campaign/window/denominator, deduplication, time zone, consent/missing populations and user journey type. Resist counting same lead through multiple channels as multiple newly acquired customers.

For example, 100 clicks and 2 confirmed leads do not establish a conversion lift without baseline, eligibility and consent coverage. If measurement access is unavailable, describe intended events and required data, not alleged performance.

## Review
Verify ownership of content rights, publication permissions, current pricing or product claims, accessibility and reading experience on target channel. Reassess distribution periodically; stop stale cross-posting that confuses users or duplicates brand statements.
