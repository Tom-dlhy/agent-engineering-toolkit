# Editorial program and measurement

**Read when:** building a production-ready content calendar, content briefs, governance or an evaluation dashboard for published content.

## Publication state machine

Proposed → researched → outlined → drafted → fact-checked → approved → published → evaluated → updated/retired. Every transition has an owner. Treat external publishing and irreversible brand claims as explicitly authorized actions, not an implicit follow-on from drafting.

## Reusable editorial brief

~~~yaml
reader_question: "How do I complete the service request?"
audience: "Existing customer"
goal: "Successful self-service completion"
primary_intent: "how-to"
evidence:
  - "Verified public form and current service policy"
sections:
  - "What information is needed?"
  - "What are the steps?"
  - "What can go wrong?"
primary_action: "Open current request form"
author: "Assigned owner"
reviewer: "Subject matter expert"
refresh_trigger: "Service policy or form changes"
~~~

This is an illustrative template, not a claim that the example service exists.

## Cadence and channel

Choose cadence based on capacity to verify and maintain content, not an arbitrary weekly quota. Plan owned site/help-center first; newsletters or social distribution only when audiences actually use them. Reusing a piece on different channels requires platform-specific editing and clear canonical strategy, not dozens of near-identical pages.

## Outcome measurement

- Workflow quality: publication lead time, review rejections, stale/unowned articles.
- Reader benefit: completed task, successful product discovery, lower repeated confusion; validate support deflection without hiding dissatisfaction.
- Acquisition: search clicks/impressions and qualified inbound when actual connected tools provide data; segment branded and nonbranded.
- Business: conversions assisted and quality of leads; label attribution windows/limitations.
- Operational debt: refresh backlog, broken external links, incorrect policy claims.

Define numerator, denominator, cohort and observation period **before** making outcome claims. With little traffic, prefer qualitative usability review over spurious A/B significance.

## Negative cases and maintenance

What if the content becomes incorrect due to pricing, product, regulation, shipping terms, framework versions or security guidance? Keep a source and update trigger. Remove or redirect retired content thoughtfully. Do not invent customer quotes, case-study results or SEO ranking improvements.
