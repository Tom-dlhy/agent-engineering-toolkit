# Content strategy

## Scope

Decide what to publish, for whom, why, in what sequence and how to maintain it. This is an editorial discipline, not the act of automatically drafting a large volume of generic SEO pages.

## Decision procedure

1. Inspect real positioning, existing product information, approved audience research, support questions, current pages and measurable business objectives. Label evidence versus assumption.
2. Map audiences to jobs, search/social/discovery intent, journey stage, objections and proof needed. Avoid invented personas.
3. Cluster related customer questions, identify gaps, compare with current content and prioritize differentiated evidence over keyword volume.
4. Draft a production plan: message, channel, source, expert reviewer, owner, CTA, update trigger, editorial calendar.
5. Define outcome metrics: useful visits, task completion, qualified actions, assisted conversions; do not confuse pageviews with business impact.
6. Check factual sourcing, copyright, consent for case studies and disclosure before publication. No automatic publishing.
