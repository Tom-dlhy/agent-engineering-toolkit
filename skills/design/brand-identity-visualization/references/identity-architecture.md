# Brand identity visualization — concept strategy

**Read when:** creating or differentiating a brand world or translating positioning into a constrained identity brief.

## Work backwards from meaning
Capture brand category, intended audience, promise, distinguishing proof, tone, trust/seriousness level and real-world touchpoints. Read and preserve existing style guide when user requests a refresh rather than rebrand. Identify what the brand should *not* evoke and what visual language competitors already own.

Derive a constrained system:
- **Symbol:** one conceptual metaphor that explains the product or feeling. Prefer meaning and recognizability over visual complexity. A monogram can be justified if its geometry carries a recognizable idea.
- **Typography:** primary function (UI legibility / expressive editorial / wordmark), type family mood, letter spacing and small-size requirements. Check real font license; do not assume reference fonts are distributable.
- **Color:** roles (core identity, secondary, surfaces, typography, warning) and their combinations, not just five attractive swatches. Validate semantic contrast in actual UI separately.
- **Image direction:** source, subject, framing, light, art direction, diversity and geographic authenticity. Replacing real local places or employees with invented imagery requires labeling.
- **Graphic grammar:** shape language, spacing, strokes, pattern, photographic overlays and distinctive composition.
- **Application:** logo/icon, website hero, product UI, packaging/signage/social. The same identity should remain recognizable across contexts.

## Three-option exploration without style lottery
Propose at most three *meaningfully* different routes, each with a brand claim, symbol rationale, palette roles, typography, supporting visuals and anti-patterns. Avoid offering twenty vaguely named aesthetics. Choose direction based on audience/constraints, and clearly label unapproved exploration as exploratory.

## Rejection tests
Could this logo be mistaken for a competitor? Does an icon remain distinguishable at 24 px? Can it work in one ink color? Will the palette pass important UI contrasts? Does the mark use an unsupported cultural symbol? Is the presentation disguising weak fundamentals with textures and mockups? Can assets be recreated in a vector editor?
